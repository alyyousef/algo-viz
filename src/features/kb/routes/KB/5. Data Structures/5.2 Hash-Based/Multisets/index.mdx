A standard Set strictly destroys duplicates (inserting `"Apple"` five times results in exactly one `"Apple"`). A **Multiset** (also known as a Bag) is a data structure that allows duplicates, but mathematically compresses them by tracking their frequencies.

## 1. The Key-Value Compression
Instead of storing `["Apple", "Apple", "Apple"]` as a massive Array (which consumes vast amounts of memory), a Multiset implements itself as a Hash Map where the Key is the item, and the Value is an integer representing its count:
`{"Apple": 3}`.

When you add another `"Apple"`, the Multiset instantly looks up the key in $O(1)$ time and mathematically increments the integer to $4$.

## 2. Fast Frequency Analytics
Multisets are the absolute standard for generating rapid statistical distributions. If you scrape 10 million words from a Wikipedia article, dumping them into a Multiset mathematically generates a perfect word-frequency distribution in $O(N)$ time. 
You can instantly query `count("the")` and receive the exact frequency in $O(1)$ time, a process that would take a standard Array $O(N)$ time to count manually.

## 3. Multiset Mathematics
Multisets support complex combinatorial logic:
- **Sum**: Adding two Multisets together mathematically adds their frequencies. (`{A: 2} + {A: 3} = {A: 5}`)
- **Intersection**: mathematically takes the *minimum* frequency of the shared elements. (`{A: 2} ∩ {A: 3} = {A: 2}`)
