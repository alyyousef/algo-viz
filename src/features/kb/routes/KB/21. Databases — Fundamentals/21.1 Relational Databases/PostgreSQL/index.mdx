PostgreSQL (often pronounced "post-gress") is a powerful, open-source object-relational database system with over 35 years of active development that has earned it a strong reputation for reliability, feature robustness, and performance.

<Callout icon="info" title="ACID Compliance">
  Postgres is fully ACID compliant (Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, Durability), making it highly reliable for financial and enterprise transactions.
</Callout>
