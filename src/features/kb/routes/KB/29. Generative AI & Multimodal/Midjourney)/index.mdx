Founded in 2022 by David Holz (co-founder of Leap Motion), **Midjourney** is an independent, self-funded AI research lab based in San Francisco. 

Despite having a fraction of the budget and employees of massive tech giants like Google and OpenAI, Midjourney has consistently maintained its crown as the premier AI image generator in the world, renowned for its unparalleled artistic quality, lighting, and cinematic composition.

## The Midjourney Philosophy (Default to Beauty)

The biggest difference between Midjourney and its competitors (DALL-E, Stable Diffusion) is its foundational philosophy: **Default to Beauty**.

If you give DALL-E the prompt *"a dog"*, it will generate a very generic, boring stock photo of a dog. DALL-E is designed to be literal.
If you give Midjourney the prompt *"a dog"*, it will generate a breathtaking, cinematic, volumetric-lit portrait of a dog shot on a 35mm lens with perfect color grading. 

Midjourney achieves this through **Aggressive Fine-Tuning on Aesthetics**. The developers spend vast amounts of time and resources training the model specifically on artistic theory, composition, and human aesthetic preferences (which they gather through massive rating systems from their users).

## The Discord Interface

For the first two years of its existence, the only way to access Midjourney was through the chat app **Discord**. There was no website and no API.
Users had to join the official Midjourney Discord server, type `/imagine` followed by their prompt into a public chat room, and wait for a bot to reply with their images.

While incredibly clunky for enterprise use, this was a genius growth hack:
1.  **Massive Social Learning:** Because all generations were public, users constantly learned advanced prompt engineering by reading what other users were typing. 
2.  **Free RLHF Data:** Every time a user upscale a specific image in the Discord channel, it provided Midjourney with free Reinforcement Learning data on which images humans preferred, allowing them to rapidly fine-tune the model's aesthetics.

## Key Features and Commands

Midjourney relies heavily on command-line parameters to control the model:
*   `--ar 16:9` (Aspect Ratio): Forces the model to generate widescreen or cinematic shapes.
*   `--stylize 1000` (Stylize): Tells the model how aggressively it should apply its own "artistic flair" to your prompt.
*   `--cref` (Character Reference): A massive breakthrough that allows users to upload a photo of a specific person's face and force the AI to maintain that exact face across dozens of different generated scenes, solving the consistency problem for comic book and game developers.

<Callout icon="scale-unbalanced" title="The Lack of an API">
Midjourney notoriously refuses to release an official API. If a developer wants to integrate Midjourney into their own software, they have to use incredibly fragile "Discord Scraper" bots that violate Midjourney's Terms of Service. This has forced almost all enterprise software to integrate DALL-E 3 instead, despite Midjourney having vastly superior image quality.
</Callout>
