While Text-to-Image models (generating an image from a blank canvas) grab all the headlines, **Image-to-Image (Img2Img)** is the workflow actually used by professional artists, architects, and game developers.

Img2Img allows you to take an existing image—like a rough pencil sketch, a 3D block-out, or a photograph—and force the AI to use it as the foundational structure for the final generation.

## How Img2Img Works mathematically

To understand Img2Img, you must first understand standard Text-to-Image Generation.
In standard generation (using a Diffusion model), the AI starts with a canvas of 100% random mathematical noise (television static). Over 50 steps, it removes that noise to create a picture based on your prompt.

**Img2Img intercepts this process.**
1.  **The Input Image:** The user uploads a rough pencil sketch of a sword.
2.  **Adding Noise:** The software mathematically adds a specific amount of noise to the sketch. 
    *   If you set the **Denoising Strength** to `0.2` (20%), the AI adds only a tiny bit of static. The sketch is still clearly visible.
    *   If you set it to `0.8` (80%), the AI adds massive amounts of static. The sketch is almost completely destroyed, leaving only a vague shape.
3.  **The Reverse Process:** The AI takes this noisy canvas and runs its standard denoising loop, guided by your text prompt (*"A glowing magical sword, 8k render"*).

Because the starting canvas was not 100% random static, but rather a noisy version of your sketch, the final image perfectly retains the composition, perspective, and general shape of your original drawing.

## The Magic of Denoising Strength

The entire art of Img2Img relies on tuning the **Denoising Strength** parameter (usually a slider from 0.0 to 1.0).

*   **Low Strength (0.1 - 0.3):** The AI acts like a photo-retoucher. It will barely change the image, perhaps just altering the lighting or enhancing the texture. Perfect for upscaling.
*   **Medium Strength (0.4 - 0.6):** The sweet spot. The AI will completely repaint the image, turning a crude MS Paint sketch into a photorealistic masterpiece, while strictly maintaining the original shapes.
*   **High Strength (0.7 - 0.9):** The AI goes rogue. It will use your input image merely as a vague color palette and generate an entirely new image that vaguely resembles the original layout.
