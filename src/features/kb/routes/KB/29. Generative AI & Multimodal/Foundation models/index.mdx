The term **Foundation Model** was coined by the Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (HAI) in 2021. It describes a massive AI model that is trained on a vast quantity of unlabeled data (usually the entire internet) at a staggering scale.

Models like GPT-4 (OpenAI), Claude 3 (Anthropic), Llama 3 (Meta), and Gemini (Google) are Foundation Models.

## The Paradigm Shift

Before Foundation Models, AI was highly specialized. If a hospital wanted an AI to read X-rays, they had to hire engineers to build an AI *specifically* for reading X-rays from scratch. This was incredibly expensive and difficult.

The Foundation Model paradigm changes this. 
Instead of building a specialized model from scratch, a massive tech company spends \$100 Million to train a single, gigantic, generalized model on everything humans have ever written. 

This model (the Foundation) is not an expert at X-rays. But because it has read every medical textbook on Wikipedia, it has a deep, underlying understanding of anatomy and biology. 
The hospital can then take this massive Foundation Model and **Fine-Tune** it for just \$5,000 using a few hundred X-rays. The resulting model will vastly outperform the AI built from scratch.

## Characteristics of a Foundation Model

1.  **Massive Scale:** They are trained using tens of thousands of GPUs (like NVIDIA H100s) on petabytes of data.
2.  **Unsupervised Pre-training:** They learn by playing a simple guessing game (like guessing the next word), meaning the data does not need to be manually labeled by humans.
3.  **Emergent Abilities:** Because they are so large, they randomly develop skills they were never explicitly taught. For example, GPT-3 was trained purely to predict the next English word, but researchers discovered it could suddenly translate French and write Python code.
4.  **Omni-use:** A single Foundation Model can be used to write a poem, debug a server, or act as an NPC in a video game.

<Callout icon="building-columns" title="The Economics of AI Infrastructure">
Training a state-of-the-art Foundation Model is so astronomically expensive (often costing over \$1 Billion in compute alone) that only about 5 companies in the world have the resources to do it. Because of this, Foundation Models have become the new "infrastructure" of the tech industry. Just as millions of startups rely on Amazon Web Services (AWS) for hosting, millions of AI startups now rely entirely on OpenAI or Anthropic's Foundation Models to power their apps.
</Callout>
