If Kinematics is the pure geometry of movement, **Dynamics** is the brutal reality of physics.

Dynamics answers the question: *"Exactly how much electrical torque must the shoulder motor output to lift a 10-pound weight if gravity is pulling it down?"*


<Callout icon="warning" title="The Nonlinear Nightmare">


  Robotic Dynamics is mathematically terrifying because it is highly nonlinear.

  If the robot is swinging its arm fast, the centrifugal force mathematically pulls the arm outward. The Coriolis force twists the arm sideways. The elbow motor must continuously mathematically calculate and counteract the momentum generated by the shoulder motor, or the entire arm will swing wildly out of control.


</Callout>


## Forward vs Inverse Dynamics

- **Forward Dynamics:** (Simulation). Given the torque applied to the motors, what will be the resulting acceleration and movement of the robot?
- **Inverse Dynamics:** (Control). Given the desired acceleration and movement, exactly how much torque must be applied to the motors right now to achieve it, counteracting gravity and friction?

Engineers mathematically solve Dynamics using the **Newton-Euler equations** or the **Lagrangian mechanics** formulation, which calculates the difference between kinetic and potential energy in the system.
