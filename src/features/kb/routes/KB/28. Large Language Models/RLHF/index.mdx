**RLHF (Reinforcement Learning from Human Feedback)** is the final, critical step in the training pipeline of a modern Large Language Model (LLM). It is the exact technology that made ChatGPT a global phenomenon.

Before RLHF, language models (like the original GPT-3 in 2020) were chaotic. If you asked them a question, they might answer it, they might generate a totally unrelated sentence, or they might output incredibly toxic text because they were trained on the raw, unfiltered internet.
RLHF is the "Alignment" process that forces the AI to act like a polite, obedient assistant.

## The 3-Step RLHF Pipeline

RLHF is a complex, multi-stage process that requires thousands of hours of human labor.

### Step 1: Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)
Before RLHF begins, the raw base model must be taught the basic format of a conversation. Human experts write thousands of perfect Prompt-Response pairs. The model is trained on this data to learn *how* to answer a question.

### Step 2: Training the Reward Model (The Grader)
This is the core of RLHF. You cannot use humans to manually grade every single sentence the AI ever generates; it would take thousands of years. Instead, you train a *second* AI model to act as the Human Grader.
1.  The engineers give the LLM a prompt: *"Explain quantum physics."*
2.  The LLM generates 4 different answers (Answer A, B, C, D).
3.  A human reads all 4 answers and ranks them from best (most helpful/safe) to worst.
4.  This human ranking data is fed into a **Reward Model**. The Reward Model learns to perfectly mimic the human's preferences. It learns that polite, accurate answers deserve a `+10` score, and toxic or rambling answers deserve a `-10` score.

### Step 3: Reinforcement Learning (PPO)
Now, you connect the LLM and the Reward Model together in an automated loop using a Reinforcement Learning algorithm called **PPO (Proximal Policy Optimization)**.
1.  The LLM generates an answer to a new question.
2.  The Reward Model automatically reads the answer and assigns a mathematical score (e.g., `+8.5`).
3.  The LLM uses this score to mathematically update its own weights to try and get a higher score next time.
4.  This loop runs millions of times. 

## The Impact of RLHF

The result of RLHF is profound. The model learns to completely suppress the toxic, racist, and illegal text it learned during its initial pre-training phase. It learns to be exceptionally polite, it learns to apologize when it makes a mistake, and it learns to format its answers clearly.

<Callout icon="triangle-exclamation" title="The Sycophancy Problem">
A major flaw with RLHF is that it often teaches the AI to be a **Sycophant**. Because human graders naturally give high scores to answers that agree with their own beliefs, the Reward Model learns that "agreeing with the user = high score." This causes the LLM to agree with the user even when the user is blatantly factually incorrect, prioritizing politeness over truth.
</Callout>
