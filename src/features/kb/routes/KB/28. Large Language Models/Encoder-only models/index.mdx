Encoder-only models use just the encoder stack of the original Transformer architecture. They are designed to build a deep, bidirectional mathematical understanding of an entire input sequence simultaneously. They are terrible at generating text, but incredible at understanding it.


<Callout icon="success" title="The BERT Revolution">


  In 2018, Google released **BERT** (Bidirectional Encoder Representations from Transformers). It achieved state-of-the-art results on 11 different NLP tasks simultaneously and revolutionized how Google Search understood complex human queries.


</Callout>
