<ConceptTemplate title="Encoder-Decoder Models">

The Encoder-Decoder model is the exact architecture proposed in the original 2017 "Attention Is All You Need" paper. It combines an Encoder stack to understand the input and a Decoder stack to generate the output.

<Callout icon="tip" title="T5 (Text-to-Text Transfer Transformer)">
  Google\'s T5 model is the most famous modern example. It frames every single NLP task (translation, summarization, classification) as a text-to-text problem. You feed the encoder "translate English to German: That is good" and the decoder generates "Das ist gut".
</Callout>

## How it Works

1. **The Encoder:** Reads the entire input sequence using unmasked bidirectional attention and compresses it into a dense mathematical representation.
2. **Cross-Attention:** The Decoder generates the output one token at a time. During generation, it uses a special "Cross-Attention" layer to continuously look back at the dense representation generated by the Encoder.
3. **The Output:** The Decoder outputs the final sequence autoregressively.
