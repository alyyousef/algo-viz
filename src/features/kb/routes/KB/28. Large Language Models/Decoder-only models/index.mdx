<ConceptTemplate title="Decoder-Only Models">

Decoder-only models use just the decoder stack of the Transformer architecture. They are autoregressive, meaning they predict the very next token in a sequence based only on the preceding tokens. This is the architecture that powers the modern Generative AI revolution.

<Callout icon="info" title="The GPT Paradigm">
  OpenAI bet heavily on the Decoder-only architecture with the Generative Pre-trained Transformer (GPT) series. They discovered that if you make a decoder-only model massive enough and feed it enough text, it develops emergent abilities like reasoning, coding, and translation without specific fine-tuning.
</Callout>
