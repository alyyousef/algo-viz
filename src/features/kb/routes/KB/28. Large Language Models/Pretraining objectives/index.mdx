Pretraining is the first, most expensive, and most compute-heavy phase of creating an LLM. Because human-labeled data is too expensive, models are trained using **Self-Supervised Learning** on trillions of words scraped from the internet. The Pretraining Objective defines the exact mathematical game the model plays to learn language.


<Callout icon="warning" title="Compute Cost">


  Pretraining a state-of-the-art model like Llama 3 or GPT-4 requires tens of thousands of GPUs running for months, costing tens of millions of dollars in compute and electricity.


</Callout>
