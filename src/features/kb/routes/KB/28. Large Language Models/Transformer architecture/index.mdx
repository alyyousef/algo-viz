The **Transformer** is a type of neural network architecture invented by Google researchers in the 2017 paper *"Attention is All You Need"*. It is the undisputed foundation of the modern AI revolution.

Every major AI model today—from ChatGPT to Claude to Midjourney to Tesla's Self-Driving system—is built using the Transformer architecture.

## Why the Transformer Changed Everything

Before 2017, AI processed language using Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and LSTMs. These older networks were fundamentally flawed because they processed data **sequentially**. 
They had to read word 1, then word 2, then word 3. Because of this, they couldn't take advantage of massive parallel processing (GPUs), and they "forgot" the beginning of a paragraph by the time they reached the end.

The Transformer threw away sequential processing entirely. Its brilliant innovation was **processing every single word in a document simultaneously** using a mechanism called **Self-Attention**. 
Because it processes everything at once in massive matrices, it scales perfectly across thousands of GPUs, allowing engineers to build models with hundreds of billions of parameters.

## The Two Halves of a Transformer

The original 2017 Transformer was built for translation (English to French) and contained two distinct halves:

### 1. The Encoder
The Encoder reads the input sentence (English) all at once. Its job is to figure out the context. It uses Self-Attention to mathematically link every word to every other word, creating a massive, highly complex mathematical representation of the sentence's meaning.
*   *Legacy:* Google used the Encoder-half to build **BERT**, which revolutionized search engines and text classification.

### 2. The Decoder
The Decoder receives the mathematical context from the Encoder and generates the output sentence (French), but it does so **Autoregressively** (one word at a time). It guesses the first French word, then looks at what it just guessed, and guesses the second word.
*   *Legacy:* OpenAI threw away the Encoder entirely. They took just the Decoder-half, scaled it up to a massive size, and created the **GPT** family (Generative Pre-trained Transformer). Almost all modern chatbots are "Decoder-Only" Transformers.

<Callout icon="gears" title="The Internal Layers">
If you zoom in on a single block of a Transformer, it contains two main sub-layers:
1.  **Multi-Head Attention:** The part that figures out how words relate to each other.
2.  **Feed-Forward Network (FFN):** A standard, massive neural network that acts as the model's "memory," storing the actual facts and logic it learned during training.
</Callout>
