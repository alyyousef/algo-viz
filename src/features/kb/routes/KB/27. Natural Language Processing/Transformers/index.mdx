Introduced by Google in the 2017 paper *"Attention Is All You Need"*, the Transformer is a deep learning architecture that relies entirely on a mechanism called **Self-Attention** to draw global dependencies between input and output.


<Callout icon="tip" title="Why it matters">


  Before Transformers, NLP relied heavily on Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and LSTMs. Transformers process entire sequences simultaneously rather than sequentially, enabling massive parallelization and the creation of Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT-4.


</Callout>
