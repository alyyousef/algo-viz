<Callout icon="info" title="Redirect">
The explicit construction of **Ontologies** (formal naming and definition of the types, properties, and interrelationships of entities) was a cornerstone of Semantic Web and Good Old-Fashioned AI (GOFAI).

In modern data stacks, strict ontologies have largely been replaced by dense vector representations and implicit relationships learned by Neural Networks. Please navigate to the upcoming **Machine Learning** modules for modern knowledge representation.
