**Face recognition** is usually three models: **detect** the box (and landmarks), **align** (warp to a template), **embed** (ArcFace / AdaFace / a vendor SDK), then **compare** with cosine. **Verification** is 1:1 ("is this the same person as the enrolled photo?"). **Identification** is 1:N (search a gallery). Those have different ROC stories and different ethics.

## 1. Deep Dive and Mechanics

**Detect.** RetinaFace, SCRFD, YOLO-face, MediaPipe. Tiny faces and extreme yaw still break cheap detectors.

**Align.** Five-point similarity transform to a canonical 112×112. A 10° roll leftover costs more accuracy than a slightly older backbone.

**Embed.** Metric learning with angular margins (ArcFace) so identities are separable on the sphere. Train on huge (and often legally ugly) datasets. At serve: L2-normalise, store 512-D.

**Match.** Threshold τ on cosine. Set τ from a target FAR on a *your-demographics* set. One global τ from LFW will not transfer.

**Liveness.** Print and replay attacks. Texture, challenge-response, or depth. Recognition without liveness is a poster attack.

<Callout icon="error" title="This is high-risk biometric processing">
Many jurisdictions restrict live facial ID in public spaces. Accuracy is worse on darker skin and women in older systems (NIST FRVT — check the latest). If you do not have a lawful basis and a bias eval, do not build this.
</Callout>

## 2. Mathematical / Theoretical Foundation

ArcFace: softmax on `s cos(θ + m)` for the true class. Verification: FAR, FRR, EER, TAR@FAR=1e-4. Identification: rank-1, TPIR vs FPIR. Open-set ID must reject unknowns, not force a nearest neighbour.

## 3. Real-World Implementation

```python
# Conceptual
box, kps = detect(frame)
face = align(frame, kps, size=112)
z = normalize(embed(face))
score = float(z @ z_enrolled)
allow = score >= tau
```

Use a maintained insightface / vendor SDK; do not train ArcFace on scraped photos.

## 5. Interview Prep

**Q: Why not classify with softmax over employees?**
**A:** Closed set, retrain on every hire, no unknown reject. Embeddings + ANN + a reject threshold scale.

**Q: Why alignment?**
**A:** The embedding is not fully pose-invariant. Alignment is cheap invariance.

**Q: How do you eval fairly?**
**A:** Stratify by sex, age, skin tone, capture device. Publish FAR/FRR per slice. Use NIST-style protocols, not a selfie demo.

## 6. Production Use Cases

- **Device unlock** (on-device, 1:1, liveness).
- **Secure workplace** where law and union rules allow.
- **Photo app** grouping (opt-in, on-device preferred).

<Callout icon="tip" title="Prefer on-device 1:1">
Unlocking a phone with a local embedding is a different moral and legal object than a city-scale 1:N camera grid. Design the smallest system that solves the job.
</Callout>
